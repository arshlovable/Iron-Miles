Delete Your Iron Miles Account

Last Updated: September 2026

Iron Miles is a mobile app developed by SharkForge Studio. This page explains how to permanently delete your Iron Miles account and associated data. You do not need to sign in on this website.

How to delete your account

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Iron Miles app and sign in.
  2. Tap the settings gear on the home screen.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Review what will be deleted, then tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Your account and associated Iron Miles data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@ironmiles.app from the email address on your Iron Miles account. Use the subject line Delete my Iron Miles account. SharkForge Studio will delete the account and associated app data.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Iron Miles account, including name, email, and sign-in details
  • Driver profile, goals, and equipment preferences
  • Generated and saved workouts
  • Workout history and Iron Miles earned
  • Fuel and meal log entries

Data that may be kept

Deleting your Iron Miles account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions, or billing may continue.

Purchase and billing records held by Google Play or our subscription provider (RevenueCat) may be retained by those services under their own policies. Iron Miles does not keep a copy of your profile, workouts, history, or fuel logs after deletion.
